# LinkedIn Project Copy

Use these six as the first public projects on LinkedIn and GitHub.

## Customer 360 Analytics Platform

Designed a modular Customer 360 analytics foundation covering contacts, companies, deals, support tickets, product usage, and lifecycle signals. The platform replaced complex one-off reporting logic with reusable transformation layers, governed definitions, documented ownership, and automated validation checks.

## SaaS Account Health Scoring

Built a daily account health scoring pipeline that unified CRM history, product usage, support interactions, deal activity, and account lifecycle signals into a trusted account-level health score for retention and expansion workflows.

## Revenue Source Of Truth

Created a unified revenue data model to reconcile differences between Finance and Marketing reporting logic, standardizing close-date definitions, deduplication, foreign exchange conversion, and revenue attribution rules.

## Data Quality And Observability Framework

Built automated quality checks for analytics pipelines focused on freshness, schema stability, duplicate detection, null thresholds, relationship checks, and metric drift before dashboard publication.

## Healthcare Readmission Risk Pipeline

Developed a synthetic healthcare machine learning pipeline that engineers readmission risk features and generates daily patient-level risk scores without using protected health information.

## Clinical Notes NLP Code Detection

Built a synthetic NLP pipeline to process clinical-style notes and identify candidate diagnosis codes missing from billed-code records, with outputs designed for human review.
