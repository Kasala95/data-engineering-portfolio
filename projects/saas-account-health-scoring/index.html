SaaS Account Health Scoring

A daily account scoring pattern for customer retention, renewal risk, and expansion prioritization.

Business Problem

Customer success and revenue teams need account signals inside their operating rhythm, not just after-the-fact dashboard views. This project shows how product usage, support burden, satisfaction, renewal timing, and annual revenue can be combined into a practical health score.

Architecture

Account signal ingestion: usage, tickets, CSAT, ARR, renewal date
Feature normalization and weighted scoring
Health band assignment: at risk, watch, healthy
CRM-ready output for customer-facing prioritization

Implementation Details

  • Scores 80 synthetic accounts using a transparent formula designed for operational explainability.
  • Rewards active usage and satisfaction while penalizing high support burden.
  • Includes renewal timing as a prioritization factor rather than a standalone churn prediction.
  • Produces a CSV output that can be plugged into a CRM enrichment or dashboard workflow.

LinkedIn Project Summary

Built a daily account health scoring pipeline that unified customer activity, support interactions, renewal timing, and account value into trusted account-level signals for retention and expansion workflows.
