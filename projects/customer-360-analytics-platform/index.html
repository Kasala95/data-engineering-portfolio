Customer 360 Analytics Platform

A modular analytics warehouse pattern for CRM entities, customer intelligence, and self-service reporting.

Business Problem

Customer reporting often breaks down when contacts, accounts, deals, and support records are modeled independently. Analysts rebuild similar joins, metric definitions drift, and dashboard consumers lose trust when the same customer has different answers in different places.

This project models a cleaner Customer 360 foundation using synthetic CRM data and a reusable mart that consolidates customer identity, revenue, contact coverage, and support workload.

Architecture

Raw CRM objects: companies, contacts, deals, tickets
Standardized entity joins and relationship checks
Customer-level mart with revenue, contacts, and support indicators
Dashboard-ready output for analysts and business teams

Implementation Details

  • Generates synthetic CRM objects across 30 companies and related contacts, deals, and support tickets.
  • Creates a customer mart with contact counts, open ticket counts, industry, and closed-won revenue.
  • Includes a SQL model showing the warehouse transformation pattern in a dbt-style layout.
  • Keeps all data public-safe while preserving the shape of real SaaS analytics work.

LinkedIn Project Summary

Designed a modular Customer 360 analytics foundation covering contacts, companies, deals, support tickets, product usage, and lifecycle signals. The project demonstrates reusable modeling, business-readable definitions, and reliable self-service analytics patterns.
