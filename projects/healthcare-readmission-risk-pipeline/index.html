Healthcare Readmission Risk Pipeline

A synthetic clinical analytics workflow for feature engineering, risk scoring, and batch model outputs.

Business Problem

Clinical teams need risk signals early enough to support discharge planning and follow-up workflows. This project recreates the data engineering side of a readmission model using fully synthetic data and transparent scoring logic.

Architecture

Synthetic patient records with clinical-style risk factors
Feature engineering for admissions, labs, medications, and social risk
Interpretable risk scoring and risk-band assignment
Daily scoring output for clinical workflow integration

Implementation Details

  • Creates 120 synthetic patient records with no protected health information.
  • Scores risk using age, prior admissions, abnormal labs, medication count, and social risk flag.
  • Assigns low, medium, and high bands for operational triage.
  • Frames the work as data engineering and ML infrastructure, not medical decision automation.

LinkedIn Project Summary
