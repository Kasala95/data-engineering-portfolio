Revenue Source Of Truth Model

A finance-grade modeling pattern for consistent revenue logic, currency conversion, and reconciliation checks.

Business Problem

Revenue reporting becomes fragile when Finance, Marketing, and executive dashboards apply different close-date rules, currency timing, duplicate handling, or attribution logic. This project demonstrates a controlled revenue model designed around consistency and auditability.

Architecture

Deal records and exchange-rate reference data
Closed-won filtering and deal-level deduplication
Standardized FX conversion into USD
Recognized revenue output and reconciliation report

Implementation Details

  • Processes 100 synthetic deal records across USD, EUR, and GBP.
  • Recognizes 56 closed-won deals with standardized FX conversion.
  • Produces a reconciliation report with recognized revenue of $3,616,925.85.
  • Documents controls that map to real finance analytics review patterns.

LinkedIn Project Summary

Created a unified revenue model to align Finance, Marketing, and executive reporting around consistent close-date logic, deduplication, foreign exchange conversion, and automated reconciliation controls.
